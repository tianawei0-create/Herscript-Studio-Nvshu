Film Introduction / 短片介绍

The Invisible Link is an AI-generated short film inspired by Nüshu, a rare writing system created and used by women in Jiangyong, China. Rather than simply introducing Nüshu as a historical script, the film explores the emotional meaning behind it: how women used writing to express loneliness, courage, memory, and support when their voices were often unheard.

The film connects past and present through two female characters from different times. One lives in a traditional world where women’s voices are restricted; the other is a modern girl facing pressure, competition, and self-doubt. Across time, a Nüshu letter becomes an invisible bridge between them.

Through poetic visuals, quiet narration, and AI-generated cinematic scenes, the film asks how forgotten female voices can be remembered in the digital age. It presents Nüshu not only as cultural heritage, but also as a symbol of connection, empathy, and women supporting women.

《看不见的连结：她的声音穿越时间》是一部以女书文化为灵感创作的 AI 短片。女书是中国江永地区女性创造并使用的独特文字系统。本片并不只是介绍女书这种古老文字，而是希望探索女书背后的情感意义：在女性声音常常被忽视的时代，她们如何通过书写表达孤独、勇气、记忆与彼此支持。

影片通过两个不同时代的女性角色，将过去与当下连接起来。一位生活在传统社会中，面对女性表达受限的处境；另一位是现代女孩，在竞争、压力与自我怀疑中寻找力量。跨越时间，一封女书信成为她们之间看不见的桥梁。

通过诗性的影像、安静的旁白和 AI 生成的电影化场景，影片思考在数字时代中如何重新看见那些被遗忘的女性声音。它呈现的女书不仅是一种文化遗产，更是一种关于连接、共情与女性互助的精神象征。

About HerScript Studio关于 HerScript Studio

A cultural learning studio for Nüshu, storytelling and interactive heritage.一个面向女书、故事叙事与互动文化学习的创意工作室。

HS

HerScript Studio is an independent student-led cultural design project. It presents Nüshu through a bilingual website, digital learning tools, visual storytelling and a board-game prototype.HerScript Studio 是一个学生主导的独立文化设计项目，通过双语网站、数字学习工具、视觉叙事与桌游原型介绍女书。

01 Cultural Focus文化主题

Nüshu, the women’s script from Jiangyong, Hunan, and the social meanings connected with women’s expression, memory and mutual support.聚焦湖南江永女书，以及它所承载的女性表达、记忆传递与互助关系。

02 Project Format项目形式

A bilingual website supported by stories, a dictionary, tracing practice, listening resources, interactive games and a physical board-game concept.以双语网站为核心，结合故事、字典、临摹、聆听资源、互动游戏与实体桌游概念。

03 Learning Aim学习目标

To make Nüshu easier to access for young audiences while keeping the presentation respectful, clear and culturally grounded.让年轻受众更容易接触女书，同时保持表达的尊重性、清晰性与文化依据。

What We Do项目内容
Mission项目使命

To help young audiences encounter cultural heritage as something they can understand, discuss and experience.帮助年轻受众以可理解、可讨论、可体验的方式接触文化遗产。

This project does not aim to replace academic research or official museum archives. Instead, it acts as an accessible entry point: a designed learning experience that encourages curiosity, empathy and further exploration.本项目并不替代学术研究或官方博物馆档案，而是作为一个易进入的学习入口：通过设计化体验激发好奇、共情与进一步探索。
